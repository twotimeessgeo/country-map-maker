## 2026-09-24 local source inspection

`scripts/extract-local-stats.py` reads the following original files under `~/Documents/New project 8/data_downloads/` and emits the small, committed JSON extracts. The originals stay outside this repository.

- `kosis_age_sex_202412.json`: `kosis/raw/DT_1B04005N/101_DT_1B04005N_M_2024.csv`, 2024-12, 5-year age rows, total/male/female persons. The 0–14, 15–64 and 65+ groups and 20–39 sex ratios are summed from raw counts before division. Official table: https://kosis.kr/statHtml/statHtml.do?orgId=101&tblId=DT_1B04005N
- `kosis_manufacturing_2024.json`: `kosis/raw/DT_1FS1101/101_DT_1FS1101_Y_2024.csv`, 2024, 17 provinces and national total, manufacturing sector C and two-digit C10–C34 sectors. Scope: establishments with 10+ employees. Suppressed `X` cells remain null. Shipments are million KRW in the extract. Official table: https://kosis.kr/statHtml/statHtml.do?orgId=101&tblId=DT_1FS1101
- `keei_regional_energy_2024.json`: `keei/2025_지역에너지통계연보_2024자료.xlsx`, sheets `Ⅰ-2` and `Ⅰ-3`, 2024p rows 254–270, unit 1,000 toe; sheets `Ⅰ-4`, `Ⅰ-5`, `Ⅴ-1` for regional totals. Supply and production source shares divide each province's fuel amount by that province's total. National totals can include other areas. Source: https://www.keei.re.kr/board.es?mid=a10306000000&bid=0015
- `knrec_regional_renewable_2024.json`: `knrec/2024_신재생에너지보급통계_통계표1_연도별지역별현황.xlsx`, sheet `2.1.1 에너지생산량(지역별)_2024` row 9 in toe and sheet `2.2.1 발전량(지역별)_2024` row 7 in MWh. Province shares divide by the workbook's nationwide total. Source: https://www.knrec.or.kr/biz/pds/statistic/list.do
- `ei_energy_2024.json`: `energy_institute/EI_Statistical_Review_2025_ALL_data.xlsx`, `TES by fuel` and `Elec generation by fuel` sheets, 2024 columns; supply unit EJ and generation unit TWh. Korea's generation shares divide the seven fuel categories by the `Total` cell. Source: https://www.energyinst.org/statistical-review
- `kosis_cultivated_area_2025.json`: `kosis/korea_land_farm_2024/raw/kosis_bubble_cultivated_area_536_info_20260822.json`, `mobInfoNewSeries` 2025 provincial values, unit ha. This source supplies cultivated area; it contains no land-area denominator or paddy/field split. Official table: https://kosis.kr/statHtml/statHtml.do?orgId=101&tblId=DT_1EB001
- `faostat_production_2024.json`: `faostat/raw/Production_Crops_Livestock_E_All_Data_Normalized_20251231.zip`, 2024 wheat, rice, maize production and harvested area (t, ha), and cattle, sheep, pig stocks (`An`, animals). Directly streamed from the normalized CSV inside the ZIP by `scripts/extract-faostat.py`; missing values are excluded. Continental records are FAOSTAT published aggregates. Ranking filters out `China, mainland` when using the `China` total, avoiding a duplicate country. Source: https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/#data/QCL
- `faostat_trade_2024.json`: `faostat/raw/Trade_CropsLivestock_E_All_Data_Normalized_20260724.zip`, 2024 wheat, maize and rice trade, export/import quantity in t. Rice trade views use the published `Rice, paddy (rice milled equivalent)` aggregate, which includes processed rice and is not divided by paddy production to form an export percentage. Published continent totals include trade within the continent. Source: https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/#data/TCL
- Religion aggregation uses `data/country-stats.js` `religion2020.counts`, Pew Research Center 2020 country counts transmitted through Our World in Data. The page marks these totals as sums of covered countries, rather than Pew's independently published continent totals. Existing local source pointer: https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/share-of-population-by-religious-affiliation.csv?v=1&csvType=full&useColumnShortNames=false
- `kosis_employment_2025.json`: `kosis/raw/DT_1DA7E33S_NEW/행정구역_시도__산업별_취업자_2025.csv.csv`, 2025 annual column, unit thousand persons. Uses `계`, agriculture A, mining/manufacturing BC, and D–U social infrastructure/services rows. The source places Gwangju and Jeonnam under a newly merged parent; their separate subrows are used for 2025 and the merged subtotal is excluded. Official table: https://kosis.kr/statHtml/statHtml.do?orgId=101&tblId=DT_1DA7E33S_NEW
- `kostat_farm_households_2024.json`: `kostat/2024_farm_households/2024_agriculture_forestry_fishery_survey_20250417.pdf`, PDF pages 18 and 46. Page 18's full-time/part-time shares aggregate all special and metropolitan cities, including Sejong, then report nine province groups. Page 46 lists each of 17 provinces' farm count and under-0.5-ha farm count. The sum of the rounded provincial estimates differs from the national estimate by two households. Extracted and validated by `scripts/extract-farm-pdf.py`. Source: https://sri.kostat.go.kr/boardDownload.es?bid=226&list_no=436097&seq=3
- `ei_energy_2024.json` also contains 2024 `Renewables Generation by Source`, `Oil Production - tonnes`, `Oil Consumption - Tonnes`, `Gas Production - Bcm`, `Gas Consumption - Bcm`, `Coal Production - EJ`, and `Coal Consumption - EJ` sheet values. Per-country production less consumption is labelled a supply balance, not net exports, because stock and transformation effects remain. Source: https://www.energyinst.org/statistical-review
- `kosis_crop_area_2025.json`: `kosis/browser_extract/crop_area_2025_20260920/source_cells.json`, KOSIS 2025 rice, vegetable, fruit and facility crop area tables DT_1ET0012, DT_1ET0013, DT_1ET0014, DT_1ET0017; and `kosis/browser_extract/DT_1ET0040/DT_1ET0040_total_land_utilization_rate_2025_20260904.json`, the 2025 overall cultivated area. Selected nonoverlapping source cells are summed where the source has crop subtypes. Source: https://kosis.kr/statHtml/statHtml.do?orgId=101&tblId=DT_1ET0040
- `molit_province_area_2024.json`: `kosis/korea_land_farm_2024/raw/molit_cadastral_yearbook_2024_12/99_summary_2024_12.xlsx`, sheet `(11)전국시군구별면적순위현황`, 252 municipal/district cadastral area rows summed by 17 provinces. The area total is 100,459.9 km²; population density divides the same December 2024 registered population by this area. Source: https://stat.molit.go.kr/portal/cate/statMetaView.do?hRsId=24
- `wpp2024_continent_age_2025.json`: `un_desa/wpp2024/raw/WPP2024_PopulationByAge5GroupSex_Medium_20260906.csv.gz`, six geographic region rows (`LocTypeID=2`), medium variant, 2025, 5-year age groups in thousands. Age groups are summed before converting to percentages. Source: https://population.un.org/wpp/
- `wpp2024_country_migration_rate_history.json`: `un_desa/wpp2024/raw/WPP2024_Demographic_Indicators_Medium.csv.gz`, `CNMR` (net migration rate per thousand) for 1990, 2000, 2010, 2020 and 2025 country rows. Source: https://population.un.org/wpp/
- `un_migrant_stock_2020.json`: `un_desa/undesa_pd_2020_ims_stock_by_sex_destination_and_origin.xlsx`, `Table 2` destination regions and `Table 1` destination–origin pairs, both sexes, 2020. The six geographic destination totals sum to the workbook world total of 280,598,105. This is migrant stock, not migration flow. Source: https://www.un.org/development/desa/pd/sites/www.un.org.development.desa.pd/files/undesa_pd_2020_ims_stock_by_sex_destination_and_origin.xlsx
- `molit_gyeonggi_land_use_2024.json`: `kosis/korea_land_farm_2024/raw/molit_cadastral_yearbook_2024_12/41_gyeonggi_2024_12_1.xlsx`, sheet `6.시군구별 지목별 면적 현황`, 2024-12-31, selected ten Gyeonggi municipalities. The seven cadastral categories sum to each municipality's total area, verified by `scripts/extract-gyeonggi-land.py`. Source: https://stat.molit.go.kr/portal/cate/statMetaView.do?hRsId=24
- `wits_export_groups_2023.json`: `wits/export_summarytext_2023/{TUR,KAZ,ARE,TUN}_2023_summarytext.txt`, WITS total exports and complete 16-product-group chart values for 2023. The public dry-region view uses only Turkey and Kazakhstan, both listed in its major-country set. Stored groups sum to about 100% after source rounding. Extracted by `scripts/extract-wits-summary.py`. Source: https://wits.worldbank.org/CountryProfile/en/Country/TUR/Year/2023/Summarytext
- `aies_state_manufacturing_2023.json`: `aies2023/AIES31BASIC02.dat` with `AIES31BASIC02_FIELDS.txt` and `AIES31BASIC02_README.txt`, 50 U.S. states, 2023 manufacturing `NAICS 31-33` and disclosed three-digit industry shipments. Input unit is $1,000; public output uses billion dollars. District of Columbia and suppressed industry values are excluded. Extracted by `scripts/extract-aies-states.py`. Source: https://data.census.gov/table/AIESBASICTIMESERIES.AIES31BASIC02
- `wto_africa_export_groups_2021.csv`: `wto/trade_profiles_2023/africa_export_commodity_groups_2021.csv`, verified against the official Ethiopia PDF `ET_e.pdf`. Four available countries (DR Congo, Ethiopia, South Africa, Botswana), 2021 exports broken into agriculture, fuel/minerals, manufactures and other. The shares may differ from 100% by 0.1 point because of published rounding. Source: https://www.wto.org/english/res_e/statis_e/daily_update_e/trade_profiles/ET_e.pdf
- `mafra_north_south_2023_2024.json`: `mafra/2025_agriculture_food_main_statistics.pdf`, PDF pages 574–575 (South/North cultivated, paddy, dry field and food-crop area, latest 2023) and 576–577 (food-crop, milled rice and maize production, 2024). The extraction script validates the transcribed values against the actual PDF page text. The ministry reproduces North Korea statistics from KOSIS; the edition's publication year is not the data year. Source: https://kass.mafra.go.kr/newkass/cmm/fms/FileDown.do?atchFileId=FILE_000000000022023&fileSn=0
